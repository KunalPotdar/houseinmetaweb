HouseInMeta is a SaaS platform for connected orbital 3D experiences of real estate assets. We bring the development, its surroundings and individual homes into one interactive journey, helping buyers understand a property from every scale.

For developers, architects and real estate teams, we turn project plans into visual experiences that support presentations, remote discovery and conversations with future buyers. Tell us about your project to shape the right combination of 3D models, home catalogues and immersive visits.
