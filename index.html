QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What is HouseInMeta?

HouseInMeta is a SaaS platform that turns real estate projects into interactive 3D experiences. It combines orbital 3D development models, 3D home catalogues and immersive interior walkthroughs that run in a web browser.

What is an orbital 3D property model?

An orbital 3D model is an interactive representation of a property development. Buyers can view the buildings and their surroundings from aerial and pedestrian viewpoints, then move on to individual floors and homes.

What is a 3D home catalogue?

A 3D home catalogue is a collection of house designs that buyers can explore in 3D, from the exterior of each house to its furnished floor plan, so they can compare options and find the right fit.

What are immersive walkthroughs?

Immersive walkthroughs let buyers look around realistic 3D interiors, room by room, in their browser before construction begins.

Who is HouseInMeta for?

Home buyers, interior designers and architects can upload a plan to explore realistic interiors, space occupancy and real-time sunlight exposure through immersive 3D experiences. Property developers and real estate agencies can also present homes before they are built in the UK, USA, English-speaking Europe, Dubai and India.

How do I start a project?

Use the contact form to tell us about your development. We will help you choose the right combination of 3D model, home catalogue and walkthrough.

Can I turn my floor plan into an immersive 3D walkthrough?

Yes. Upload your plan at app.houseinmeta.com to experience your future home in 3D within minutes. Explore realistic interiors, space occupancy and real-time sunlight exposure.

Do I need to install software to explore my home in 3D?

No. HouseInMeta immersive 3D walkthroughs run in your web browser. Open app.houseinmeta.com to upload your plan and get started.

How can interior designers and architects use HouseInMeta?

Interior designers and architects can use immersive 3D walkthroughs to review interiors, understand space occupancy and explore sunlight exposure with clients before construction.
