About

Interactive 3D property experiences for developers, architects and home buyers.

Who we are

HouseInMeta brings together orbital 3D property models, home catalogues and immersive walkthroughs. Explore a development from the site overview to individual homes, or bring a floor plan to life in your browser. Explore our solutions or discuss your project.

Explore at three scales

An orbital model shows how buildings, outdoor spaces and access routes fit together. A home catalogue helps visitors compare individual homes and their layouts. An interior walkthrough gives a closer view of rooms, circulation and possible furnishing choices. These experiences answer different questions and can be combined for one project.

Use visualizations alongside project documents

Rendered furniture, finishes, landscaping and sunlight are illustrative unless the project documentation states otherwise. Confirm dimensions, specifications, availability and pricing with the developer or relevant professional before making a property decision.

Start with your project

Tell us the property type, location, audience and the part of the project you want people to explore. We can discuss the suitable presentation and the plans or visual references needed.
