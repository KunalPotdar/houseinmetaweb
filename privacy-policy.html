1. Scope of this policy

This policy describes the HouseInMeta marketing website at www.houseinmeta.com, including its articles, project demonstrations and enquiry form. The separate application at app.houseinmeta.com and external services may have additional privacy information. Review that information before uploading plans or creating an account there.

What happens when you visit?

You can browse this website without creating an account or providing your name, email address or phone number. We receive those details only if you choose to send them through an enquiry or email.

Optional Google Analytics is off unless you choose to allow it. Google AdSense is integrated only on our blog article pages, where ads may appear depending on Google's ad-serving settings and applicable consent requirements. Hosting and content providers still receive the technical information needed to deliver the website, such as your IP address and the resources your browser requests.

2. Who to contact

HouseInMeta

For questions about this website or a request concerning your information, email houseinmeta@gmail.com. Please describe your request without sending passwords, payment details or identity documents in your first message.

3. Information we receive

  • Enquiries you submit: your name and email address, selected project type, any optional phone number or message you provide, and the submission time. Visiting the contact page without submitting the form does not send these form details to us.
  • Email correspondence: information you choose to send when discussing a project, requesting a demonstration or applying for a role.
  • Technical information: hosting and content providers receive information needed to deliver a page or asset, such as your IP address, browser details and the requested resource.
  • Optional analytics: if you allow it, Google Analytics receives information about visits to the homepages and careers page, including page, device and browser information, and uses cookies to distinguish visits. This involves browsing data even though you are not asked to provide your name or email address.

You do not need to submit an enquiry or accept analytics to read the website.

4. How information is used

We use enquiry details to respond to your request, arrange demonstrations and discuss services. This can involve steps you ask us to take before entering a contract. Technical information supports delivery, troubleshooting and security of the website. Optional analytics helps us understand which pages visitors use and is enabled only after your choice to allow it.

An enquiry is not consent to advertising cookies or a newsletter. Do not include sensitive personal information in the project description.

5. Service providers and external links

Website hosting, asset delivery, enquiry processing and email providers process information needed to provide those functions. Our contact form sends submitted details to a Google Apps Script endpoint to process the enquiry. Google also processes optional analytics if you enable it. These providers may process information in countries other than your own; their privacy information describes their processing and transfer arrangements.

Project demonstrations may load images, models or other resources from external hosting services. Following links to the separate HouseInMeta application, WordPress articles or other websites takes you to services with their own privacy practices.

For Google's practices, read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.

6. Google Analytics and cookie choices

Why we use analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, to understand visits to our homepages and careers page and improve the website. When enabled, it can process page views, visit timing, browser and device characteristics, cookie identifiers and approximate location. These identifiers can distinguish visits even when you have not provided your name or created an account.

Google receives technical information when your browser connects to its services. Google explains that, for users in the EU, Switzerland and the UK, Analytics uses IP addresses to derive approximate location and then discards them before logging. Read Google's regional Analytics privacy information.

Our website's analytics configuration disables Google Signals and advertising personalization. It does not send contact-form fields as analytics events. Contact enquiries are processed separately as described above.

Consent and browser storage

Optional Google Analytics is off by default. If you allow it below, it will load on subsequent visits to our homepages and careers page. This choice does not enable advertising or Google advertising personalization.

You do not need to accept analytics to browse, read articles, explore demonstrations or send an enquiry. If you do nothing, analytics stays off unless you previously allowed it on this browser. When you save a choice, we remember it locally on your device so you do not have to choose again on each visit.

Cookie expiry and the retention of data already sent to Google are different. Deleting a cookie does not delete historical Analytics records. To ask about information already processed or make a privacy request, contact houseinmeta@gmail.com.

7. Google AdSense and advertising choices

Advertising on blog articles only

Our blog article pages include Google's AdSense script. Our homepages, contact and privacy pages, service pages and interactive project viewers do not include this advertising script. Loading a blog page can connect your browser to Google even if no ad is displayed. Your analytics choice above does not authorize advertising cookies.

How AdSense uses information

Google and participating advertising providers may use cookies and similar technologies to deliver ads, measure their performance and limit repeated displays. For personalized advertising, Google and its partners may use earlier visits to this website and other sites to select relevant ads. This can happen without a visitor submitting a form.

Non-personalized ads use context rather than past browsing behavior for targeting, but can still use cookies for reporting and limiting repeated ads. They are not automatically cookie-free or exempt from consent. See Google's explanation of personalized and non-personalized ads.

Advertising consent and controls

Consent-dependent advertising requires choices separate from analytics, including a way to refuse and later withdraw consent. Personalized ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland require a Google-certified consent management platform identifying participating providers and their privacy information. The analytics buttons on this page do not provide advertising consent, and merely browsing or submitting an enquiry does not constitute consent.

You can also manage Google's personalized advertising through My Ad Center and learn about Google's advertising technologies. Some other providers offer choices through the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out tool. These controls do not replace this website's consent choices and may apply only to a particular browser or account.

8. Retention and security

Retention depends on the information and the service involved. Contact us to ask what enquiry or project information is held about you, how long it is needed, or to request deletion. Some records may need to be retained to meet contractual or legal obligations. The analytics preference on this browser expires after 180 days.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Please send only information needed for your enquiry and contact us before sharing confidential project documents.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent; this does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

Send requests to houseinmeta@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify that a request concerns your own data. You can also contact your local data protection authority, such as the CNIL in France, or find an EU authority through the European Data Protection Board.

10. Children and policy updates

This website presents property visualization services for adults and professional teams. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

We will update this page when the website's data practices change. A policy update or continued browsing is not consent to optional analytics or advertising.
